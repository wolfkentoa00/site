Garlic-Butter Chicken Ramen

Chef-Grade Elevation • No Dairy • Double Serving

Prep 5 Min
Cook 8 Min
Yields 2 Bowls

Mise en Place

The Base

Aromatics & Fats

Seasoning Balance

Method

1

Bloom the Aromatics

In a medium pot over medium heat, melt the 2 tbsp butter. Add the minced garlic and the white parts of the scallions. Sauté for 60–90 seconds until fragrant and pale gold. Do not burn.

2

Build the Compound Broth

Pour in 4 cups of water and increase heat to high. Stir in the soy sauce, brown sugar, and both chicken seasoning packets. Bring to a rolling boil.

3

Cook Noodles

Add both noodle blocks to the boiling broth. Cook for exactly 2 minutes. (The noodles should be slightly underdone as they will sit in hot broth).

4

The "Butter Baste" Poach

Reduce heat to a gentle simmer (medium-low). Crack the 2 eggs carefully into the broth alongside the noodles. Cover the pot and let steam for 3 minutes for jammy yolks.

5

Finish & Serve

Divide noodles and broth between two bowls using tongs. Top each with an egg. Drizzle 1 tsp sesame oil over each bowl and garnish with the remaining green scallions.

Chef's Notes

  • Spice Level: If you want heat, add Chili Crisp after plating to preserve the chicken broth flavor.
  • Protein: If adding rotisserie chicken, toss it in during Step 3 to warm it through.
