Cheesy Black Bean Enchiladas

Zero chopping required. A rich, flavorful vegetarian dinner using simple pantry staples. High reward, low effort.

Prep Time 10 min
Cook Time 20 min
Yields 8 Enchiladas

Ingredients

  • 2 (15oz) cans black beans, rinsed & drained
  • 1 (28oz) can red enchilada sauce
  • 3 cups Monterey Jack cheese, grated
  • 1 (4oz) can diced mild green chiles (optional)
  • 8-10 flour tortillas (soft taco size)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

Flavor Hack: Is your canned sauce bland? Whisk in a pinch of cocoa powder and a splash of vinegar before using. It adds instant depth!
  1. Preheat: Set oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 9x13 inch baking dish. Pour about ½ cup of the enchilada sauce onto the bottom of the dish to coat it.
  2. Make the Filling: In a medium bowl, combine the rinsed black beans, canned green chiles (if using), cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, and 1 ½ cups of the cheese. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper. Stir well.
  3. Assemble: Lay out a tortilla. Spoon about 1/3 cup of the bean mixture in a line across the center. Roll it up tightly. Place it seam-side down in the baking dish. Repeat with remaining tortillas.
  4. Sauce & Cheese: Pour the remaining enchilada sauce evenly over the rolled tortillas. Make sure the edges are covered so they don't dry out. Top with the remaining 1 ½ cups of cheese.
  5. Bake: Bake uncovered for 20–25 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and the sauce is bubbling around the edges.
  6. Serve: Let cool for 5 minutes before serving. Optional: Top with sour cream or cilantro if you have them.
